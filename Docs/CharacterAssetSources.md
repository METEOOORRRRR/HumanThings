describing their license as "CC 4.0". Neon Outrider was subsequently supplied in

The owner also supplied the five HAZE/KAI/NOVA/REI/ASH GLBs and corresponding
concept sheets, authorizing separate texture repaints while preserving originals.
Ashen Sentinel is KAI; Wasteland Sentinel is ASH (verified by rendered appearance).

The GLB metadata contains no copyright or license field. The exact Creative
Commons license identifier, attribution details and source/export URLs have not
been supplied or independently verified. This provenance note does not replace
the applicable license or grant additional rights. Record those details here
when the owner provides the original license/export information.

Project-derived assets include in-place looping animation clips, controllers,
gameplay prefabs, HumanThings material variants, mipmapped texture copies and
region/weather masks. The original GLB files are preserved unchanged.

The separately imported Synty map assets are not covered by this character
provenance note and are excluded from version control.
